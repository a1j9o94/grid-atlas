# The Los Angeles anchor is wrong and is retracted

Recorded 2026-08-19. The second anchor in this project to encode a contestable
assumption, and both were written by me.

## The claim, and where it came from

The reader brief and the Map III release gate both carried "Los Angeles County must be
served by somebody" as an anchor. The blind reader on the western states read Los
Angeles County, FIPS 06037, as **blank**: ink 0.09, the plate's lightest level,
confirmed on a contrast-stretched crop of the whole county interior showing bare paper,
with a clean boundary and no mesh-offset ambiguity. It marked the read `firm`.

## Why the blank is correct and the anchor is not

Map IV's own title is *fields of operations of principal power groups*. Its legend
carries 24 marks, and every one of them is a named holding company or group: Electric
Bond and Share, Middle West Utilities, The United Corporation, Duke Power, Pacific Gas
and Electric, and so on. There is **no Southern California Edison cell, no Los Angeles
municipal cell, and no municipal category at all**.

Los Angeles in 1932 was served by the city's own Bureau of Power and Light, which was
municipally owned, and by Southern California Edison. Neither is a principal power
group, and neither has a mark on this plate. So no legend key could fill Los Angeles
County even in principle, and blank is the only reading the plate's own scope permits.

The anchor did not test the trace. It tested an assumption about who counts as a power
group, and it would have failed a correct read while passing any reader who forced a
fill there.

## The replacement

**The Sacramento Valley reads `pacific-gas-electric`.** Pacific Gas and Electric is
legend cell p18, so the plate can express it, and the blind reader found it at 06105
and across much of northern and central California. That is a real test of the trace
rather than a test of a premise.

## The pattern worth keeping

Two anchors now, both mine, both broken the same way. Jefferson County Alabama asserted
Southeastern Power and Light where Birmingham Electric was an EBASCO company. Los
Angeles asserted a holding-company group where the operators were a municipal and an
independent.

**An anchor has to be a claim the source can express.** Before adding one, check that
the legend has a cell capable of satisfying it. Both failures came from reasoning about
history and then asserting it against a plate whose scope is narrower than history.

## A note on an earlier verdict

The first automated attempt's post-mortem cites "Los Angeles read fill_share 0.26" as
evidence its served layer was wrong. The premise it was judged against was this same
anchor. That does not make attempt one's served layer right, its threshold sensitivity
of plus or minus 13 points stands on its own, but Los Angeles was not the evidence
against it that the memo took it for.
